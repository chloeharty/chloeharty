## Hi I'm Chloe Harty 👋

I'm a Software Engineering student at the University of Victoria with a passion for problem-solving, user-centered design, and building technology that has a meaningful real-world impact. I recently participated on the WEST & Fraser Health's pediatric MRI simulator project. Within this project our wonderful team designed a software system to control the MRI simulator. The overarching goal of the project was to help ease children's anxiety around receiving an MRI. Our system was developed to run locally using a raspberry pi using a touchscreen to control the lights and sounds experienced during the simulation. Within the first semester, I began the project focused on UX, creating Figma wireframes, presenting designs to our clients, incorporating their feedback, and documenting the frontend through a user manual to support a successful handoff.<br>
In the second semester, I transitioned into a leadership role, growing the software team and restructuring development into three layers: frontend, backend logic, and hardware integration. To support cohesive development, I implemented an Agile framework, coordinated industry mentorship, led weekly meetings as both Scrum Master and Product Owner, and established a well-documented GitHub backlog. This experience strengthened my skills in software architecture, project leadership, UX design, and cross-functional collaboration. This project taught me how to build not just software, but an effective process for developing it as a team. Alongside my WEST project work, I've developed experience with Python, C, Java, Git, embedded systems, automated testing, and UI/UX design.<br>
I am so grateful for the experience I have gained with WEST and the skills that I have learned at the University of Victoria. As a young developer, I am always eager to build a new project and continue my professional growth.<br>
